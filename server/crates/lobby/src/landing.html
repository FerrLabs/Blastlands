Pick your character

Each one starts with a different kit and carries one ability on a cooldown. Characters play in Arena mode.

Demolisher

Starts with one more tile of blast reach.

6 sTrigger: sets off your oldest live bomb right now.

Runner

Starts two speed steps faster.

10 sVanish: hidden for two seconds wherever you stand.

Grenadier

Cluster bombs that flare past the edge of the blast.

5 sThrow: your next bomb lands up to three tiles ahead.

Sapper

Bombs that burn through soft blocks.

10 sWall: raises a block in front of you for eight seconds.

Three ways to play

The host picks the mode. Soft blocks grow back, and after ninety seconds the board starts closing in, so no round drags on.

The original

Classic

The bomberman board: 13 by 11, a pillar on every other tile, one corner each. Your bombs come back once they go off, and placing one is the only move you have.

No sight

Classic Blinded

The same board and the same bombs, but you only see the players you have a line to. Every pillar becomes somewhere to hide.

Characters

Arena

An eroded island with cover in clumps and bushes to hide in. Bombs lie on the ground and are spent for good. Dash, shove and use your ability, and only see who you have a line to.

Controls

Keyboard and mouse, or any Xbox or PlayStation pad.

Move WASD ZQSD Arrows
Drop a bomb E
Dash Space
Shove Left click
Ability Right click

On a pad: stick to move, A or Cross to drop, a shoulder to dash, X or Square to shove, Y or Triangle for the ability.
