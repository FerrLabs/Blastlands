The sound effects in this folder are built from three Kenney packs, all released
under Creative Commons Zero (CC0): https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

- Impact Sounds 1.0      https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds
- Sci-fi Sounds 1.0      https://kenney.nl/assets/sci-fi-sounds
- Interface Sounds 1.0   https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds
