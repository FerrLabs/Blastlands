Blastlands

A multiplayer bomber for up to eight players, with bots to fill the empty seats. Last one alive wins the round.

What's in it

Characters

Each one starts with its own bonus and has an ability on a cooldown. They only exist in Arena: the Classic modes play without them.

Demolisher

Starts with one more tile of blast reach.

Trigger6 s cooldown

Sets off your oldest live bomb right now.

Runner

Starts two speed steps faster.

Vanish10 s cooldown

Hidden for two seconds wherever you stand.

Grenadier

Cluster bombs that flare past the edge of the blast.

Throw5 s cooldown

Your next bomb lands up to three tiles ahead.

Sapper

Bombs that burn through soft blocks.

Wall10 s cooldown

Raises a block in front of you for eight seconds.

Modes

The host picks one when creating the match. Destroyed blocks grow back after a while, and at ninety seconds the board starts shrinking, so rounds stay short.

Original

Classic

The board everyone remembers from bomberman: 13 by 11, a pillar on every other tile, a corner each. Your bombs come back after they explode. No dash, no shove, just bombs.

Hidden players

Classic Blinded

Classic, except you can't see a player unless nothing stands between you. With a pillar on every other tile, that is most of the time.

With characters

Arena

A bigger island with bushes to hide in. Bombs are picked up off the ground and gone once used. You can dash, shove people around and use your character's ability, and like in Classic Blinded you only see the players you have a line to.

Playing online

Launch the game, type a name and you land on the match list. Host a match and it shows up there for everyone else. Add bots if your friends are late, then press Start when you are ready. Matches run on our servers, not on someone's PC, and if a player drops out a bot finishes the round for them.

Controls

Keyboard and mouse, or an Xbox or PlayStation controller.

Move WASD ZQSD Arrows
Drop a bomb E
Dash Space
Shove Left click
Ability Right click

On a pad: stick to move, A or Cross to drop, a shoulder to dash, X or Square to shove, Y or Triangle for the ability.
